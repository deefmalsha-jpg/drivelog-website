Privacy Policy

DriveLog by Deef – Business Mileage Tracker

Effective date: November 18, 2026

DriveLog by Deef (“DriveLog,” “we,” “our”) is a mobile application for recording business mileage, driving sessions, and vehicle-related expenses. This policy explains the information the app uses and the choices available to you.

1. Information you enter

The app may process information you choose to provide, including your name or profile details, selected business or delivery platform, odometer readings, trip dates and times, notes, expense descriptions and amounts, and other mileage or expense records.

2. Location information

With your permission, DriveLog uses your device’s location to measure GPS distance during driving sessions. Location access may be requested while the app is in use and, where enabled and supported, in the background during an active tracking session. You can change location permissions in Android settings. Disabling location access may limit GPS-based features, but manual entries may remain available.

3. Photos, camera, and files

If you use receipt or trip-image features, the app may access photos you select, images captured using your camera, or files you choose. These images may be associated with expense or session records and included in reports you generate.

4. Notifications and device functions

DriveLog may request notification permission to display tracking-related notifications, including an Android foreground-service notification during active tracking. The app may also use device functions necessary for location tracking and generating or sharing reports.

5. Storage and sharing

DriveLog is designed to maintain mileage, expense, and image records on your device. The app does not currently offer a DriveLog account or developer-operated cloud synchronization feature. When you export or share a report, backup, or image, the destination app or service you select may receive that content and handle it under its own privacy practices. Device-level backups or services you enable may also operate independently of DriveLog.

Some platform services or third-party software components used to run or distribute the app may process technical information. This policy should be reviewed if analytics, advertising, crash reporting, remote APIs, or other data-processing services are added.

6. Why information is used

Information is used to record driving sessions, calculate distances and durations, organize business mileage and expenses, attach supporting images, display your history, and create reports and exports.

7. Retention and deletion

Records are generally retained on your device until you delete them using available app controls or remove the app’s stored data. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored app data, subject to Android backup and recovery settings. Exported files and copies shared outside the app must be deleted separately from their destinations.

8. Security

DriveLog relies in part on Android’s app-storage and permission protections. No device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock and take care when sharing reports containing location, financial, or personal information.

9. Children’s privacy

DriveLog is intended for people tracking business or work-related mileage and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through the app.

10. Your choices

You can choose what details to enter, whether to attach images, whether to export records, and whether to grant location, camera, photo, or notification permissions. You can manage applicable permissions in your device settings. Some features may not function without the permissions they need.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The revised version and effective date will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or support, email drivelog.support@gmail.com.
